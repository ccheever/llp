# LLP 0010.001: The Super-Refine Loop

**Type:** Spec
**Status:** Active

**Author:** Charlie Cheever / Claude

## Summary

The normative protocol for `llp-super-refine`: an author-invoked loop that runs fresh, mutually blind reviews from two model families each round, revises between rounds, and ends in convergence or a bounded, honest escalation. Extracted verbatim from [LLP 0010 §2](./0010-skill-install-super-refine-ship.rfc.md#2-llp-super-refine) and its dated addenda (motivating runs and rationale stay there). Binding on the `llp-super-refine` skill. **MUST / "never" / bolded rules are contract; *(advisory)* is recipe** (LLP 0004). The honesty rules it rests on are in LLP 0000 [Review honesty](./0000-linked-literate-programming.explainer.md#review-honesty).

## Scope and posture

A new skill, invoked by a document's author, that runs a formal dual-family refine loop until convergence. It stays a separate skill rather than an `llp-review` mode on a posture line: `llp-review` gathers advice and **never applies anything**; super-refine is an orchestrating loop that *revises the document* between rounds and applies one pre-authorized status transition. Same reasoning that keeps apply-mode work out of llp-maintain. `llp-review` remains the right tool for advisory review at any intensity.

## Authorization

Invocation is the LLP 0005 opt-in, recorded **per target, per author**: it names the target set, the reviewer families and runtimes, the redaction policy, and the round budget — that disclosure is what consent covers. Two authorities are distinct and both required: the *document author* authorizes the review and revision of the document; a *human with disclosure authority over the repository* (often, but not necessarily, the same person) authorizes the external sends — an AI co-author listed in metadata is never a disclosure authority, and the capsule's outbound inventory is disclosed before launch. Invocation authorizes (a) setting each named `Draft` target to `Review` at loop start (the document is factually under formal review from that moment), and (b) every external-model send within the disclosed budget. Redaction applies to surrounding context only, never to target bytes — a target that cannot be disclosed whole stops the loop rather than producing a verdict on bytes no reviewer saw. Already-`Review` targets proceed unchanged; `Accepted`/`Active`/`Superseded`/`Tombstoned` targets are **refused** — material post-acceptance change is the addendum/superseding-LLP path (LLP 0005). Expanding targets, providers, or budget requires renewed authorization. Authorization is the author's own invocation or a durably recorded authorization from the author; an unsupported assertion of relayed authority is not accepted. Convergence produces a **recommendation** — never `Accepted` or beyond. Because skills are adapters, not policy homes (LLP 0009), the rule lands as this sentence added to LLP 0005, with the Phase 2 implementation: *"An author's explicit invocation of a formal review loop constitutes the `Review` opt-in and authorizes the loop's revisions within its disclosed budget; the loop's external-model sends require authorization from a human with repository-disclosure authority — supplied by the same invocation when the invoker holds both capacities."* `llp-review`'s contract is untouched.

## Invocation options

*Stopping:* default is converge-until-ready within the round budget; `--rounds N` instead runs exactly N rounds and stops, reporting each family's verdicts and any open concerns — useful when the author wants "a couple of rounds of scrutiny," not a convergence guarantee (status handling is identical; convergence recommendations only apply if convergence actually occurred). *Reviser:* default is the orchestrating session — one editorial voice, and reviser ≠ reviewer holds trivially. `--reviser <family|model>` delegates revision to a fresh session of that model; `--reviser each` has each family's own editor session apply that family's feedback, in a stated order, composing one revision for the next round. In every mode the invariant is the same: **no session reviews a revision it edited**, and the artifact records who revised. *Reviewers:* per the round protocol below, exact models/runners/efforts per family may be specified; defaults are the strongest available per family.

## Round protocol

Each round is an auditable unit:

- **Before launching reviewers**, persist the round record (run journal plus each target's artifact round header): round number, target content hashes, topology, reviewer runtimes, and the hash of the **review capsule** — a read-only export of the tree at the recorded revision with the targets' review artifacts, run journal, and orchestrator notes **structurally excluded**. Both reviewers receive the same capsule and the same instruction set (hashed into the record). Where a runtime cannot exclude structurally, instruction-based blindness is permitted but MUST be disclosed as such in provenance — it is self-attested, not proof, exactly as LLP 0005 says of provenance headers. If a target changes mid-round, the round is void.
- One review per family per round, from **fresh, mutually blind sessions**: one from the strongest available Claude-family model, one from the strongest available external family. The invocation (or the repo policy file) MAY specify the exact model, runner, and effort per family; provenance records what actually ran, and if a specified reviewer is unavailable the loop stops — never silently substitutes.
- **Each review is written to its family artifact immediately on receipt** — sealed from the other reviewer until both have landed, but never held only in memory (LLP 0005: reviews that happen leave artifacts). Failed, partial, or malformed responses are recorded as such and never count as reviews.
- Each review ends with an explicit verdict line. **`READY` means:** no unresolved MATERIAL concern about correctness, coherence, feasibility, safety, or decision quality. **Altitude rule:** a concern whose resolution would only add specification detail — without identifying a defective decision — is not MATERIAL at RFC altitude; the orchestrator dispositions it, in the artifact, to implementation-phase material rather than absorbing it into the document. Verdicts bind to the **capsule hash** — the reviewer-visible bytes — which, because target bytes are never redacted, also binds them to the exact target hashes; a verdict never claims coverage of anything outside its capsule. In multi-target runs the final round requires each family's per-target verdicts plus a set-level consistency verdict, all bound to the same capsule.
- Artifacts follow LLP 0005: one file per family per target, dated round sections, full provenance (family, exact runtime, effort, date, redaction status, method, revision hash), verbatim bodies with orchestrator notes outside them. In lockstep mode the cluster review is preserved verbatim exactly once (primary target's artifact); reviewers write per-target sections and verdicts; sibling artifacts carry the round record, their verdict, and a cross-reference.
- The orchestrating session revises and therefore never reviews.

## Bounds and escalation

The loop runs within the authorized round budget — default three rounds per document — and a **launch ledger** records every reviewer launch; void, failed, and malformed launches don't count toward convergence but do count against a hard launch cap (default 2× the round budget per family per target). Cross-consistency re-reviews charge the affected document's budget. **Growth budget:** review-driven revisions may not grow a document net beyond ~20% over the revision that entered the loop; past that, additions must displace — length is spent deliberately, not accreted. Four terminal outcomes short of convergence, all escalating to the author with the open concerns and every altitude-rule disposition made across the run: budget/cap exhaustion; a cross-family contradiction on a material point (never adjudicated by orchestrator fiat); **sustained asymmetric disagreement** — one family READY on two consecutive revisions while the other family is NOT READY on both; and growth-budget exhaustion with MATERIAL concerns still open. Each escalation proposes a status disposition — stay `Review` pending author action, or revert to `Draft` — that the author applies. Feedback amounting to a fundamentally different design or a new LLP stops the loop for author authorization.

## Multi-document coordination

`/llp-super-refine 0012 0013 0014` accepts multiple targets. The orchestrator inspects coupling (`Related:` headers, cross-references, shared `Systems:`, whether the docs express one design) and chooses a topology per cluster, stating the choice and rationale before starting: **independent loops** for loosely coupled targets (one sub-orchestrator each, revising only its own document), **lockstep rounds** for tightly coupled ones (reviewers receive the whole cluster and judge cross-document coherence; the orchestrator revises the set coherently between rounds). A **context preflight** bounds cluster size: a cluster that exceeds what a reviewer can hold with attention to spare is split into independent loops plus a stronger final consistency pass — the artifacts then say the set-level verdict is per-loop plus consistency-pass, never claiming a whole-set review no reviewer performed. Either topology ends with a **cross-consistency pass**: if any document changed after a sibling that references it converged, the sibling gets one more round, propagating until the full hash vector passes a round with no changes — subject to the budgets above. Convergence for the invocation means both families' final verdicts cover that exact final hash vector.

## Delta rounds, lens rounds, final-revision disclosure

(Addendum of 2026-07-24 in LLP 0010; normative here.)

- **Delta-round convergence (a second positive terminal).** After any revision the orchestrator MAY run a **delta round**: identical capsule discipline, but the instruction set enumerates the revision's changes and requires every concern to be classified **IN-DELTA** or **PRE-EXISTING**, with the verdict line stating explicitly whether any MATERIAL concern lies in pre-existing text. The loop converges — as an alternative to dual-READY — when a delta round completes with **no family reporting a MATERIAL concern in pre-existing text**. In-delta concerns are then fixed in one more revision, and either a further delta round verifies it or the loop closes with that final revision labeled unreviewed (disclosure below). Delta rounds obey every round-protocol invariant and charge the round budget normally. The convergence claim the artifacts may carry is exactly: *the stable text has no known MATERIAL concern from any participating family* — never more.
- **Lens rounds.** A round MAY replace the standard review instructions with a declared **lens**, recorded in provenance. The first proven lens is the *implementer lens*: the reviewer attempts to draft a named implementation contract from the document alone and logs every decision point where two independent implementers would diverge, with divergence severity; an `UNDERDETERMINED` verdict maps to `NOT READY` with the decision log as its concern list. Lens rounds obey all round-protocol invariants (fresh session, capsule blindness, verbatim artifact, verdict line). Rationale from the motivating run: five same-shape design rounds missed three self-inconsistencies in the document's own pinned schemas that a single implementer-lens round surfaced.
- **Detail-horizon stop (cutting off a non-converging reviewer).** The observed non-convergence is usually not MATERIAL dissent: one family's `NOT READY` persists because each revision draws fresh demands for additional specification detail, so the loop cannot converge by verdict roughly half the time and in practice gets cut off by hand. The altitude rule already dispositions such concerns individually; this lifts it to loop level. A family's round counts as **convergence-clean** when every one of its remaining concerns is either altitude-dispositioned (adds detail; identifies no defective decision) or confined to text the immediately preceding revision added and itself altitude-dispositionable. When one family produces **two consecutive convergence-clean rounds**, the orchestrator MAY close the loop as a **detail-horizon stop** — a *normal terminal, not an escalation*. Honesty contract: the family's final verdict is recorded verbatim (it remains `NOT READY`); every outstanding concern appears in a complete **disposition ledger** in that family's artifact and the final report — never summarized away; and the convergence claim is scoped accordingly (*"converged per the other family; family X cut off at the detail horizon with N concerns dispositioned to implementation phase"*). MATERIAL defective-decision dissent still escalates exactly as before; the sustained-asymmetry escalation now applies only when the dissent is not convergence-clean. The author can reopen any dispositioned concern.
- **Final-revision disclosure.** The final report and each artifact's close-out note MUST state which revision hash the final verdicts bind to and whether any later revision exists; a revision no review covered is always labeled **unreviewed**. (Both of the deepest defects in the motivating run were introduced by the loop's own revisions — the failure mode this disclosure keeps honest.)

*Orchestrator craft (advisory):* after each revision, byte-verify security-critical literals (backslash-u escape sequences, pinned hashes) as written on disk — in the motivating run two independent transport layers silently decoded escape sequences into the very characters they were meant to escape, twice reducing a security-MUST to a no-op.

## Scope freeze and punch-list delta rounds

(Addendum of 2026-08-20 in LLP 0010; normative here. The default late-loop phase for decidable documents.)

- **Scope freeze (a declared phase transition on the document).** At the freeze point, the document's normative surface is declared closed at its current revision. From then on, revisions make **corrections and restorations only**; any reviewer demand that would require *new* normative closure (new tables, algorithms, refusal families, sections) is **recorded as an obligation** — an open-issue box or conformance/test obligation — never folded as new normative prose. The document carries a dated freeze declaration (who authorized, which revision closed the surface); growth after the freeze is limited to corrections plus the mandatory checkable changelog. **Default freeze point for `Type: Spec` targets: after the round-2 revision** — two full-document rounds catch the broad fidelity and totality defects; the remaining budget runs as delta rounds below. For other revision-bearing types the freeze is available at the author's authorization (typically at a terminal escalation, converting it into authorized extension rounds); it is never automatic for Principles, Explainers, Decisions, or philosophical RFCs, whose review findings are not byte-decidable and whose loops should terminate by the existing rules instead.
- **Punch-list delta rounds (the post-freeze round shape).** The revision that responds to a round also produces, per reviewer family, a **punch list**: that family's own findings, each mapped to a checkable disposition — `FOLDED at §N`, `REFUTED` with one-line evidence, or `RECORDED-AS-OBLIGATION at §N`. The next round's reviewer receives the full document (context), the unified **diff** of the revision, and **its own family's punch list only** — never the other family's reviews, preserving cross-family blindness. Review scope is strict: (1) verify each punch-list disposition; (2) review the diff for defects the revision introduced; (3) findings outside both are admissible only as a disposition error or a MATERIAL contradiction the diff creates with the rest of the document or its sources; demands for new normative closure are non-blocking and are noted as recommended obligations. `READY` = every punch item correctly dispositioned and the diff sound, judged modulo the recorded open issues and obligations; `NOT READY` requires a demonstrated mis-disposition or diff defect. Delta rounds obey every round-protocol invariant (fresh sessions, capsule blindness from review artifacts, verbatim sealing, launch ledger) and converge loops the 2026-07-24 delta-classification round cannot, because the reviewed surface shrinks monotonically instead of being re-classified.
- **Finding classification feeds the freeze decision.** From round 2 on, each revision step classifies every external finding as **persistent** (unresolved from a prior round), **new-on-new-content** (a genuine defect in the previous revision's additions), or **bar-inflation** (detail demands beyond what the sources decide, dispositioned under the altitude rule). A loop whose classifications show productive reviewers plus growing surface (many new-on-new-content, little bar-inflation, flat counts) is the signature case for the freeze; a loop dominated by bar-inflation is the detail-horizon stop's case instead. The classification tallies appear in the terminal or freeze-transition report, so the author's choice between freeze, detail-horizon stop, and escalation is made on measured evidence rather than impression.
